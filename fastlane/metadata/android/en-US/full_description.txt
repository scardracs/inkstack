Inkstack is a modern manga reader for Android 16+, built for comfortable reading and control over your library.

<b>Read offline</b>
Download chapters and keep reading without a connection. Your library stays on your device.

<b>Sources and parsers</b>
Browse and read from built-in manga source parsers, with an extensible parser architecture for adding more catalogs over time.

<b>Sync and backup</b>
Back up and restore your library, history, and settings so you can move between devices without starting over.

<b>Scrobbling</b>
Track progress on AniList, MyAnimeList, Kitsu, Shikimori, and other supported services from inside the app.

<b>Material Expressive</b>
A polished Material Design 3 interface with expressive theming, tuned for long reading sessions.

<b>Privacy</b>
No ads. Open source under GPL-3.0. You choose what to sync and which scrobbling accounts to connect.
